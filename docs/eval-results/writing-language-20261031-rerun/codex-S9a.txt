`/sc-hints` gives **practical advice based on project evidence** and recommends the next command.

- `/sc-hints` uses the current conversation and work context.
- `/sc-hints <question>` focuses on a specific question.
- It explains the recommendation, supporting evidence, prerequisites, and expected result.
- It is **read-only**: it does not modify files, start implementation, or approve artifacts.

For example:

```text
/sc-hints My PRD is approved. What should I do next?
```

After checking the relevant documents, it may recommend `/sc-plan` to prepare the technical specification and goals.
