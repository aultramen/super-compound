# Reliable verified knowledge across retry and resume

## Problem
Verified outcomes need reusable memory and safe continuation across context loss.

## Symptoms
Copied records could inflate recurrence, compaction timestamps did not save actions, and explicit stale guidance could surface in retrieval.

## Root cause
Record counts and unstructured handoffs lacked stable observation identities, evidence provenance, and typed recovery checks.

## Solution
Use structured capture with stable origins and hashed evidence, exact-copy consolidation, strict atomic replacement, validated checkpoints and fresh ledgers, filtered retrieval, and comparable evaluation.

## Prevention
Verify before capture, preserve verified goals on retry, require three independent observed/confirmed origins for promotion, and keep unavailable host evidence unknown.
