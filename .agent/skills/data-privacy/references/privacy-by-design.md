## Privacy-by-Design Principles

Apply the seven Privacy-by-Design principles (proactive, privacy as the default,
embedded in design, positive-sum, full-lifecycle protection, transparency,
user-centric). The checks that operationalize them live in
`review-checklists.md`; the assessment shape lives in `dpia-template.md`.
Project-specific defaults: data collection and analytics are opt-in, PII is
identified at the data-model level, and deletion covers backups.
